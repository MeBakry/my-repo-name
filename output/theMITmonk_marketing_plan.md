# Marketing Launch Plan: Compounded Pharmacy Platform

## 1. The Strategy: "The Pharmacy Compliance Flywheel"
Since the **Compounded** platform is already built to automate OCP/NAPRA Risk Assessments and MFRs, our marketing must focus on **Acute Pain**: the manual documentation burden.

### Target Audience:
*   **Primary:** Designated Managers (DMs) and Owners of compounding pharmacies in Canada.
*   **Niche:** Pharmacies performing Level B and Level C compounding (highest regulatory scrutiny).

---

## 2. Phase 1: Precision Lead Generation (The Microscope)
**Tool: Apollo.io**
*   **Action:** Filter for "Pharmacy Owners," "Pharmacy Managers," and "Compounding Pharmacists" within Canada.
*   **Niche Filter:** Focus on independent pharmacies rather than large chains, as they feel the documentation burden more acutely.
*   **Goal:** Build a list of 500 high-intent prospects.

---

## 3. Phase 2: Hyper-Personalization (The Human Layer)
**Tool: Clay**
*   **Action:** Import the Apollo list into Clay to find the "Human Signal."
*   **Enrichment:**
    *   Check for OCP inspection status (if public) or recent news about pharmacy compliance.
    *   Verify if the pharmacy lists "Compounding" as a specialty on their website.
*   **Automation:** Use AI to draft a personalized opening line for each email: *"I noticed [Pharmacy Name] specializes in high-complexity compounding; how are you handling the new Level C risk assessment documentation?"*

---

## 4. Phase 3: The Visual Pitch (The Hook)
**Tool: Gamma**
*   **Action:** Create a "Pharmacy Compliance in 2026" visual deck.
*   **Content:**
    *   **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Manual PDF filling vs. Compounded’s **Deterministic Rule Engine**.
    *   **Regulatory Map:** Visualizing the shift in NAPRA standards.
    *   **The "Zero-Failure" Moat:** How our platform ensures they never miss a WHMIS or GHS hazard statement.
*   **Link:** This deck will be the primary call-to-action (CTA) in our outreach.

---

## 5. Phase 4: Nurture & Conversion (The Digital Rep)
**Tool: Brevo & Calendly**
*   **Sequencing (Brevo):**
    *   **Email 1:** The "Value First" gift—A checklist for Level B/C compliance (link to Gamma deck).
    *   **Email 2:** Case study—How a local pharmacy cut MFR creation time by 80%.
    *   **Email 3:** The Frictionless Offer—"Is your MFR database ready for an audit? Let's verify."
*   **Conversion (Calendly):** Every email includes a Calendly link for a "15-minute Compliance Audit" (Demo).

---

## 6. Phase 5: Closing & Scaling (The Glue)
**Tool: Stripe & N8N**
*   **Payment (Stripe):** Set up **Stripe Billing** for recurring monthly subscriptions ($199/mo) or a "Pay-per-Assessment" model for smaller pharmacies.
*   **Automation (N8N):** 
    *   When a new assessment is generated in the app, trigger a "Review Request" email to the pharmacist.
    *   Automate the sync between Stripe and the user's dashboard to unlock "Level C" features upon payment.

---

## 7. Cost of Launch (Monthly Estimates)
*   **Apollo:** $49 (Basic)
*   **Clay:** $185 (Launch tier for hyper-personalization)
*   **Brevo:** $9 (Starter tier)
*   **Gamma:** $12 (Plus tier)
*   **Stripe:** 2.9% + $0.30 per sale.
*   **Total Monthly Burn:** **~$255**

---

## 8. Success Metric: The "Balance Loop"
We will measure success not by clicks, but by **Assumption Testing**:
*   *Assumption:* DMs care most about speed.
*   *Test:* Does the "80% time saving" headline outperform the "Audit Protection" headline in Brevo? 
*   *Pivot:* Adjust the **Signal-to-Innovation Loop** based on which marketing message gets more Calendly bookings.
