# The MIT Monk: Detailed AI Business Roadmap (2026)

## 1. Phase 1: Building From Scratch (The "Engine" & "Admin")
Before you market, you must have a delivery mechanism. The strategy shifts from "weeks of development" to "hours of prompting."

### Key Tools for Building:
*   **Lovable:** The primary engine for app development.
    *   **Usage:** Converts plain English prompts into functional web apps with Stripe hooks.
    *   **Cost:** Free (5 credits/day); **Starter (~$20/mo)** for custom domains; **Pro (~$25-$50/mo)** for high-volume messages.
*   **Cursor / Replit:** AI-native coding environments for technical builds.
    *   **Usage:** Use AI agents to write, review, and debug code simultaneously.
    *   **Cost:** Hobby ($0); **Pro ($20/mo)** for unlimited tab completions; **Teams ($40/user/mo)**.
*   **Stripe:** The "Revenue" pillar.
    *   **Usage:** Automates payments and RevOps, saving $300k+ in headcount.
    *   **Cost:** **2.9% + $0.30** per transaction; adds 0.7% for recurring billing.
*   **Gumroad:** Your "Invisible Warehouse" for digital assets.
    *   **Usage:** Automated delivery, taxes, and receipts for e-books or courses.
    *   **Cost:** Flat **10% + $0.50** per transaction.
*   **AirTable:** Your "Process Management" hub.
    *   **Usage:** Tracks leads, deal values, and next actions without CRM overhead.
    *   **Cost:** Free (1k records); **Team ($24/seat/mo)** for 50k records; **Business ($54/seat/mo)**.

## 2. Phase 2: Marketing & Demand Generation (The "Flywheel")
Marketing in 2026 is about "Micro-targeting" and "Human Enrichment."

### The Marketing Stack:
*   **Apollo (The Microscope):** For targeted prospecting.
    *   **Usage:** Filter leads by narrow criteria (e.g., "CTOs of fintech companies in NY").
    *   **Cost:** Free (Basic access); **Basic (~$49/mo)**; **Professional (~$79/mo)** for A/B testing.
*   **Clay (The Human Layer):** Enrich leads with real-world data.
    *   **Usage:** Personalize outreach using social media profiles and press mentions.
    *   **Cost:** **Launch ($185/mo)** for 2.5k credits; **Growth ($495/mo)** for 6k credits + CRM sync.
*   **Brevo (The Digital Rep):** Lead nurturing engine.
    *   **Usage:** Automates the 6–8 "touches" required to close a deal.
    *   **Cost:** Free (300 emails/day); **Starter ($9/mo)** for 5k emails; **Standard ($18/mo)** for automation.
*   **Gamma (The Visual Hook):** Turn strategy into polished decks.
    *   **Usage:** Pitch visual content (memorable vs. text-only).
    *   **Cost:** Free (400 credits); **Plus (~$12/mo)**; **Pro (~$25/mo)** for premium models.
*   **Calendly:** Removes scheduling friction.
    *   **Usage:** One link, zero back-and-forth.
    *   **Cost:** Free (1 event); **Standard ($12/mo)** for unlimited events.

## 3. Phase 3: Scaling & Automation (The "Glue")
Use "Vibe Automation" to let the business run while you sleep.

### Automation Strategy:
*   **Zapier:** For simple, stable automations.
    *   **Cost:** Free (100 tasks); **Professional (Starts ~$30/mo)** for multi-step Zaps.
*   **N8N:** For power, cost control, and AI reasoning at scale.
    *   **Cost:** **Cloud Starter ($20/mo)**; **Cloud Pro ($50/mo)**; Self-hosted ($0).
*   **The DREAM Framework:** Map decisions to **D**emand, **R**evenue, **E**ngine, **A**dmin, or **M**arketing.

## 4. The Strategic "Moats" (Staying in Business)
1.  **Counterpositioning:** Attack incumbent models (Netflix vs. Blockbuster).
2.  **Habit Creation:** Make your product a "sticky" part of the workflow.
3.  **Learning Loops:** Use user behavior as your single source of innovation.

## 5. Summary of the "Millionaire Loops"
*   **Balance Loop:** Asymmetric Advantage vs. Acute Customer Pain.
*   **Speed to Revenue Loop:** Ship daily; Product-Market Fit is a moving target.
*   **Sweat Equity Loop:** Sweat the details to build a resilient foundation.
